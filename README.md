# Sales Performance Dashboard (Power BI)

Practice project using Microsoft's public **Financial Sample** dataset, focused on data modeling, DAX measures, and interactive dashboard design in Power BI.

## Overview

The report is structured around three angles to support business decision-making:

1. **Product & Pricing Analysis** — identifying top-selling products by volume and analyzing average sale price by category
2. **Geography & Profit** — comparing profitability by country, highlighting which markets convert sales into profit most efficiently
3. **Market Segmentation** — visualizing profit distribution across customer segments (Government, Small Business, Midmarket, Enterprise, Channel Partner)

## Tools & Techniques

- **Power BI** — interactive visualizations and dashboard design
- **DAX** — calculated measures for total sales, total profit, and average price
- **Data Modeling** — time-series structuring across the dataset's date range
- **Geographic Visuals** — map-based visuals for regional performance analysis

## Key Insights

- Clear seasonality pattern, with a profitability peak toward year-end
- Some countries generate high sales volume but convert less efficiently into profit than others — a distinction only visible once margin is analyzed alongside revenue
- Heavy concentration of profit in a single customer segment, suggesting a potential opportunity to diversify

## How to View

1. Download the `.pbix` file from this repository
2. Open it in **Power BI Desktop**
3. Use the "Year" filters at the top of the report to interact with the data
